## Inferences (not stated in the docs)

- inference: /clear most likely does not call a cache-delete API. Old entries are not referenced by the new prefix and age out after the TTL. Basis: row 25 plus "expire after a period of inactivity" (row 17). Not documented.
- inference: a /clear conversation can hit an existing entry only if its request prefix (tools, system, project-context layer, working-directory announcement, git snapshot) matches exactly and the entry is still inside its TTL. Basis: rows 8, 9, 18, 19, 20, 24.
- inference: /clear reloads CLAUDE.md (row 24), so a CLAUDE.md edit made before /clear can change the prefix and miss the cache. Basis: row 24 plus the layer table.
